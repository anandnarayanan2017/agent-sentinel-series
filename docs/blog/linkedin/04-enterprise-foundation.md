The difference between a demo and an enterprise product isn't prettier charts.

It's the questions a bank's security review asks first.

Who is allowed to call this system? Where does the data live? Who looked at what? Does a risky finding wait for a human?

So the next phase of Agent Sentinel wasn't about detecting more. It was about making what it detects trustworthy: sign-in through the firm's identity provider, and refusing requests if that isn't set up. Separate permissions for sending events, reading findings and approving them. High-severity findings waiting for a named reviewer.

I'm also explicit about what isn't done yet: team isolation, managed policy workflows, and dashboard sign-in are on the roadmap.

A roadmap you can point to is more credible than a feature list that implies everything is solved.
